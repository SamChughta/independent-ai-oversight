# Independent Oversight for Autonomous AI Agents and Physical AI

**Author:** Sam Chughtai
**Concept date:** 4 October 2026

## Why this work exists

Autonomous AI agents are moving from chat windows into real systems: they read files, call tools, move money, and increasingly steer robots and uncrewed vehicles. Most of the controls around them today are built and operated by the same people who deploy them, and most of the evidence about what an agent did is written by the agent's own software.

That leaves a gap. When something goes wrong, who can say with confidence what happened, and who could have stopped it?

## Focus of this research

- Oversight of AI agents that is genuinely independent of the agent and its operator
- Trustworthy, verifiable evidence of agent behavior for auditors and regulators
- Safe behavior for physical AI systems when things fail, including lost communications
- Practical evaluation on edge hardware (NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin) rather than only in the cloud

## Status

Active research in a private lab. Technical details are not published here.
